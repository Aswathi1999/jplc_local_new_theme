## 1. Product Overview

### 1.1 Problem Statement
JPLC needs a premium digital presence to showcase its coffee brand, products, and store locations. There is currently no dedicated website that represents the JPLC brand at the quality level expected of a premium coffee company.

### 1.2 Solution
A premium coffee brand website inspired by the design standard and user experience of Starbucks — built specifically for JPLC branding. The site will focus on brand presentation, product showcase, store discovery, and mobile app redirection. It is **not** an e-commerce platform.

### 1.3 Key Innovation
- JPLC is the parent brand of **Bean Brew** (mobile coffee app)
- The website acts as a brand and discovery hub — all ordering is funnelled through the Bean Brew mobile app
- Starbucks-quality UI/UX with JPLC-specific branding and content (no direct Starbucks copy)

### 1.4 Timeline
3–4 weeks from project kick-off

## 2. User Personas

### P1: Coffee Customer
- **Needs:** Browse the menu, find a nearby JPLC store, and download/open the Bean Brew app
- **Pain:** No current branded online presence to explore products or locate stores
- **Uses the website for:** Menu exploration, store locator, app download

### P2: First-Time Visitor / Brand Discoverer
- **Needs:** Understand JPLC's brand story, coffee heritage, and product quality
- **Pain:** No platform communicating JPLC's premium positioning
- **Uses the website for:** About section, heritage story, offers, and rewards overview

### P3: Business / Partnership Enquiry
- **Needs:** A way to contact JPLC for partnerships, franchise, or general enquiries
- **Pain:** No quick contact channel
- **Uses the website for:** Contact / enquiry form

- Premium coffee brand aesthetic — quality and feel comparable to Starbucks (do **not** copy exact Starbucks content, wording, or brand assets)
- Clean, modern, high-quality UI/UX
- Smooth animations and transitions
- High-quality coffee and product imagery
- Consistent JPLC branding across all pages
- Premium visual hierarchy and layout

**Reference websites (for style standard only):**
- https://starbucks.in/ (India)

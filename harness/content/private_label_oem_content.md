H2: Frequently Asked Questions
What is private label coffee manufacturing?
Private label coffee manufacturing is the process of producing a finished coffee product — roasted, packaged, and labelled — under your brand name, using a third-party production facility. JPLC handles the entire process from roast profile development through to retail-ready packaging at our DMCC-certified facility in Dubai.
What's the difference between private label coffee and white label coffee?
White label coffee refers to a standardised product produced for multiple brands with minimal customisation, typically a set blend in generic packaging with your label applied. Private label coffee is developed specifically for your brand — custom blend, custom roast profile, custom packaging. JPLC offers both, depending on the requirement and timeline.
What's the minimum order for OEM coffee manufacturing in the UAE?
Minimum order quantities vary by format — roasted whole bean, ground, capsule, and RTD each have different production thresholds. Contact our manufacturing team directly for volume-specific pricing and lead times.
Can JPLC help with coffee brand development UAE as well as production?
Yes. For brands at an earlier stage, we offer coffee branding and brand development support alongside the manufacturing programme, covering product positioning, visual identity direction, and go-to-market planning.
How long does it take to launch a custom coffee manufacturing UAE product?
Timeline depends on the complexity of the brief, the number of sample iterations required, and packaging lead times. A straightforward white label coffee programme can move from brief to first production run in six to eight weeks. Custom blend development with new packaging typically takes longer — our team will give a realistic timeline at the briefing stage.
